Account Management

Password Reset

  1. Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password".
  2. Enter your email address.
  3. Follow the instructions sent to your email to reset your password.

Updating Profile

  • Log in to your account.
  • Navigate to "My Profile".
  • You can update your name, email, and shipping address here.

Deleting Account

  • If you wish to delete your account, please contact support.
  • Note: This action is irreversible.

Refund Policy

At SupportAI, we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase.

Standard Refund Policy

  • You may request a full refund within 30 days of your purchase date.
  • To initiate a refund, please contact our support team with your order number.
  • Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days back to the original payment method.

Exceptions

  • Digital products that have been downloaded are non-refundable.
  • Customized items are non-refundable unless defective.

Shipping Information

Domestic Shipping

  • Standard Shipping: 5-7 business days. Free for orders over $50.
  • Express Shipping: 2-3 business days. Flat rate of $15.
  • Overnight Shipping: 1 business day. Calculated at checkout.

International Shipping

  • We ship to over 100 countries.
  • Delivery times vary by location, typically 10-20 business days.
  • Customs duties and taxes are the responsibility of the recipient.

Tracking

  • You will receive a tracking number via email once your order ships.

Troubleshooting

Login Issues

  • Ensure you are using the correct email and password.
  • Check your internet connection.
  • Clear your browser cache and cookies.
  • If the issue persists, try resetting your password.

Payment Failures

  • Check that your card details are correct.
  • Ensure your card has sufficient funds.
  • Contact your bank if the transaction is being declined.

Website Errors

  • If you encounter a 404 or 500 error, please refresh the page.
  • If the error continues, please report it to support with a screenshot.
