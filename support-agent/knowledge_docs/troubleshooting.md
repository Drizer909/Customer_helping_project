# Troubleshooting

## Login Issues
- Ensure you are using the correct email and password.
- Check your internet connection.
- Clear your browser cache and cookies.
- If the issue persists, try resetting your password.

## Payment Failures
- Check that your card details are correct.
- Ensure your card has sufficient funds.
- Contact your bank if the transaction is being declined.

## Website Errors
- If you encounter a 404 or 500 error, please refresh the page.
- If the error continues, please report it to support with a screenshot.
