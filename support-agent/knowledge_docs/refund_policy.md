# Refund Policy

At SupportAI, we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase.

## Standard Refund Policy
- You may request a full refund within **30 days** of your purchase date.
- To initiate a refund, please contact our support team with your order number.
- Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days back to the original payment method.

## Exceptions
- Digital products that have been downloaded are non-refundable.
- Customized items are non-refundable unless defective.
