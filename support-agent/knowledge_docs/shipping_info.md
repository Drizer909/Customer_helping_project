# Shipping Information

## Domestic Shipping
- Standard Shipping: 5-7 business days. Free for orders over $50.
- Express Shipping: 2-3 business days. Flat rate of $15.
- Overnight Shipping: 1 business day. Calculated at checkout.

## International Shipping
- We ship to over 100 countries.
- Delivery times vary by location, typically 10-20 business days.
- Customs duties and taxes are the responsibility of the recipient.

## Tracking
- You will receive a tracking number via email once your order ships.
